Protocol Roadmap

Our strategic journey to build the world’s leading Real World Asset ecosystem.

COMPLETED

  • Launch PANGEA Token: Distribution of initial native utility tokens and liquidity seeding.

  • Build Community & Developer Network: Onboard initial Web3 builders, validators, and advocates.

IN PROGRESS
Phase 2 — Digital Asset Layer
  • Real World Asset Registration: Standardized digital onboarding for physical assets.
  • Tokenized Ownership Protocol: Full & fractional token minting engine.
  • Fractional Asset Infrastructure: Micro-unit liquidity vaults for institutional real estate & debt.
  • Asset Metadata Engine: Oracle integration for proof-of-reserve and valuation feeds.
  • Transparency & Verification Dashboard: Real-time investor portal for asset auditing.
UPCOMING
Phase 3 — Intelligent Ecosystem
  • AI-Assisted Asset Validation: Machine learning algorithms verifying property title data.
  • Smart Settlement Engine: Atomic delivery-versus-payment (DvP) trade clearing.
  • Decentralized Identity Integration: Zero-Knowledge KYC/AML compliance attestation.
  • Enterprise API Services: B2B integration gateways for traditional asset managers.
  • Cross-Platform Asset Connectivity: Interoperable messaging relays to EVM & Solana.
UPCOMING
Phase 4 — Global RWA Network
  • Multi-Asset Marketplace: 24/7 liquid secondary market trading platform.
  • Cross-Chain Asset Interoperability: Omnichain token transfer security protocol.
  • Institutional Tokenization Solutions: Turnkey sovereign debt & corporate asset tokenization.
  • Global Liquidity Infrastructure: Deep liquidity provider pools for institutional settlement.
  • Decentralized Real Asset Ecosystem Expansion: Scaling target to $10B+ total value locked.
